About Us

We Care For Your Dental Health

Best Dental Solutions is a state-of-the-art dental clinic led by Dr. Rameezuddin T and Dr. Samiunnisa Begum S, dedicated to delivering comprehensive, affordable, and compassionate dental care in the heart of Chennai. With a strong focus on patient comfort, hygiene, and advanced technology, our clinic offers a full range of dental services—from preventive care and cosmetic dentistry to root canal treatments, orthodontics, and implants. Our experienced team ensures every patient receives personalized care in a friendly and stress-free environment. Whether you're here for a routine check-up or a complete smile makeover, we’re committed to making your visit comfortable and your smile brighter. Come experience expert dental care that puts your needs first—because at Best Dental Solutions, your smile is our priority.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Best Dental Solutions located?

Our dental clinic is at 28, Teachers Colony Main Rd, Workers Estate, Neelankarai, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600115. We see patients from Neelankarai, Palavakkam, Kottivakkam, Injambakkam, Thiruvanmiyur and across Chennai.

What are your clinic timings?

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:30 PM, and on Sunday from 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM.

Do you offer online dental consultations?

Yes. You can book a 30-minute video consultation with our dentists over Google Meet on Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM. The consultation fee is ₹299, paid by UPI. Choose "Online Consultation" in the booking section above, pick a slot, pay, and upload your payment screenshot. You'll get the Google Meet link by email straight away.

Which dental treatments do you provide?

We provide laser root canal treatment, dental implants, Invisalign clear aligners and orthodontics, smile designing, full mouth rehabilitation, oral cancer screening, children's dentistry and oral surgery, along with routine check-ups and cleanings.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment form on this page, call us on +91 96001 18079, or message us on WhatsApp.
